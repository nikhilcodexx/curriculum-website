How this track fits your day

Time. Years 1 and 2: use the optional 19:30 to 20:30 slot Monday to Thursday, plus the Saturday research hour. That is about 5 hours a week. Years 3 and 4: add the Thursday professional block and a Saturday afternoon slot, about 8 to 10 hours a week, because the projects join the main track.

If it is too much. Drop to 3 hours a week. The main software and ML track comes first, because the skills you build there are what you will use to help the planet.

Honest limit on biology. Free online learning covers theory, data and computational biology (bioinformatics). Wet-lab skills need a real lab, so plan to join an iGEM team, a university project or an internship later.

Measure real impact. Use numbers and sources, state your uncertainty, and avoid claims you cannot support. Training big models uses energy too, so prefer small models and free shared notebooks, and record the footprint of your own projects.

Start local. In my case Bihar and the Ganga basin gives me real problems to work on: air quality, floods, crop residue, rooftop solar and water. Local, measurable work beats big vague plans.
